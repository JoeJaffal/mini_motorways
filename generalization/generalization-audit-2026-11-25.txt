Correction to earlier external-baseline recommendation:
The Nature Computational Science 2023 DRL-urban-planning code supports freezing
land use and road-only planning. However, the released hlg.yaml and dhm.yaml
set skip_road=true, road_ratio=0, road_network_weight=0. Supplement section 2.1
explicitly says the real-world experiments keep roads and residential areas
and plan facilities only. The synthetic experiment plans land uses and then
branch roads. Thus road redesign on HLG/DHM is an adapted experiment requiring
baseline setup/training, not reproduction of published real-site road results.
