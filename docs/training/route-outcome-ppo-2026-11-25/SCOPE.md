# Scope of the bounded road repair

The current learner is still one Maskable-PPO actor. This lesson temporarily trains six parameter tensors in its conditional road heads and four in its task critic. Its learned Wait heads and shared visual encoder are frozen so road training cannot silently change build/Wait probabilities on the same observation. Different road actions can still produce different future observations and therefore different Wait decisions.

Code inspection: the actor receives an81-channel visible board,54 global quantities and visible motorway slots. Building types/colors and driveways are encoded explicitly; traffic features use visible car positions/headings across observations. The wide spatial module has five dilated convolution layers and a63-cell receptive field. This is learned spatial processing, not a road search. Existing connectivity/supply evidence describes visible road components; no teacher route or hidden future event is added by this lesson. Sources: training/visible_observation.py, city_wide_strategy_policy.py, city_strategy_policy.py, city_relational_policy.py, city_wait_choice_policy.py.

Important limit: freezing the encoder restricts this experiment to improving road choices from its existing representation. If practice improves little, that would not prove PPO cannot learn the task. A later experiment could preserve a frozen Wait representation while allowing a separate road representation to adapt, but that is only a hypothesis, not implemented or selected here. Conversely, a successful road lesson would still not establish traffic strategy, later-arrival prioritization or full tool selection. Native city retention and fresh confirmation remain necessary.

The original native critic and its Adam state are retained in an untouched template. The lesson uses a temporary critic for its intentionally different reward scale. Export restores the original critic before native value-based training. Actor progress is preserved; the lesson critic is also retained in the lesson checkpoint for possible resume. No restart of the learned actor is implied.
