## How to evaluate the guard

Preserve every interrupted pause for review. A changed network alone is not proof of useful construction, and repeated states can occur during legitimate revision; these are diagnostic signals rather than new masks or rewards.

Inspect interrupted traces, especially longest completed pauses. If the guard cuts off coherent construction that would normally be followed by wait,it is too restrictive for that workload. If traces mostly cycle without time progress,the guard bounds wasted compute but reveals a policy learning problem; increasing the threshold alone does not fix it. If never approached,it is an inactive safety margin,not a proven optimum. Validation of guard behavior is separate from validating the policy: completed held-out score and completion rates must still improve,with interrupted episodes disclosed.
