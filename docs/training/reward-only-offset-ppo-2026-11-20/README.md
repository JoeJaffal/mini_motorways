# Reward-only PPO for two-tile lateral offsets

The user authorized targeting the geometry that failed in the previous [clear-land test](../reward-only-clear-roads-2026-11-20/README.md#completed-pilot), with simpler routes, bends and adjacent repairs retained as rehearsal. The clear-road model previously scored 66/100 and failed all 34 two-tile offset layouts.

A read-only probability check found that, on short two-offset boards, the previous policy assigned the first action of a known working route about 6×10⁻⁸ probability at its median. Those are probabilities for scripted reference moves, so they do not prove that all possible routes are equally unlikely. At temperature 4, the corresponding median is about 0.19%. This motivates an exploration change. The scripted moves were used only in feasibility and probability checks. [Probability probe](warm-exploration.json).

The isolated host adds a distance-2 warm-up with the full two-tile sideways offset and one road unit. Eight scripted feasibility cases gave at least three deliveries. The adapter, masks, and observations matched the original host on one standard fixture from each of clear, bend and adjacent. Ordinary geometry and native rules are unchanged. [Fixture patch](short-offset-fixture.patch), [verification](verification.json).

The continuation restores the exact policy weights, Adam state, and decision counters from the previous PPO checkpoint. It applies temperature directly to the PPO action distribution, including policy likelihoods during updates. Six workers target each sign of offset and four rehearse easier clear, bend and adjacent tasks. Native seed filtering selects visible layout geometry before a decision. It starts at two tiles of span and grows one at a time up to seven only when both directions reach at least 85% over three complete rollouts at the current distance. Earlier lengths stay in the mix. The reward is 1 for any legal route that connects, otherwise 0. No teacher actions or road planner enter the rollout. Evaluation disables temperature scaling.

The first service failed before any training because the runner omitted a policy-fingerprint import. The missing import was added, and the pilot was relaunched in a fresh output directory. [Startup error](startup-failure.log).

The active retry has a cap of 131,072 additional build decisions or 30 minutes. First rollout: left 7/384, right 5/384 at the short span. Monitor with `tail -F output/training/offset-ppo-pilot-01-retry-launch/training.log`. [Configuration](config.json), [launch evidence](launch.json).

## Exploration diagnosis at the three-tile stage

The distance-2 stage passed the rolling training threshold in both offset directions and advanced to distance 3. The snapshot at the start of the longer phase still placed little probability on useful sequences. On held-out distance-3, two-offset boards, the offline verifier enumerated geometrically adjacent intermediate cells between the designated approach tiles, applied both orders of each pair of native paid edges, and retained only sequences that actually connected. At training temperature 4, the median sum of first-two-action probabilities for these verified sequences was 9.76×10⁻¹⁰. This is a lower bound on the total probability of connecting; optional free driveway actions and other sequences were not enumerated. The sampled training log also recorded almost no successes at distance 3 early in the phase. This explains scarce learning signal without attributing it to a simulator defect. The probe's checkpoint and full logs remain local. [Probability evidence](span3-exploration.json).

## Final result and early stop

The pilot unlocked three-tile routes after 17 warm-up rollouts. At distance 3, only **1 of 6,696** targeted training games connected over 16 complete rollouts, with no improving trend. I stopped just after the saved update at 198,656 cumulative decisions, or 67,584 added to the parent, in 358.80 seconds. Sixteen already-collected decisions from the next rollout were discarded. The trainer wrote its status and the service was stopped to clean up subprocesses still closing. The complete saved update was retained. [Checkpoint boundary](early-stop-boundary.json), [progress](progress.jsonl), [terminal status](status.json).

Frozen evaluation used temperature 1 in both sampled and highest-probability modes. The new distance-2 warm-up passed 100/100 in both modes (51/51 and 49/49 by side). The existing clear-land suite fell from 66/100 to 33/100 in both modes. It retained all 33 aligned cases, lost the 33 one-tile offset cases, and still missed all 34 two-tile offset cases at the standard distances. It retained 100/100 adjacent gaps and 100/100 bends. This is a short-task success with no target transfer and a measurable regression. The prior 66/100 checkpoint stays the clear-land reference. [Grouped comparison](metrics.json), [standard sampled](final-sampled.json), [standard highest probability](final-greedy.json), [warm-up sampled](final-short-sampled.json), [warm-up highest probability](final-short-greedy.json).

Recommended next experiment, pending approval: keep the parent's reliable checkpoint, use the same connectivity-only reward, and give the earliest two-build offset layouts more exploration room during training (for example, temporary surplus road stock). Then return to the original budget for held-out checks. A reward change would be a separate user decision. No additional training was started. The prior and new checkpoints, including the exact early diagnostic snapshot, remain local.
