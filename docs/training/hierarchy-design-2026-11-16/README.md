## Discussion framework, not adopted design

Separate three questions: what a persistent goal represents; which primitive edits/waits the lower policy chooses; when the higher level changes goals. First compare goal vocabularies before picking numbers of networks or an algorithm.

1. **Explicit game goals:** choose a goal kind plus target entities/area, such as serving a destination or relieving a congested area. Interpretable, but handcrafted goal types can exclude useful strategy or leak engineering through success tests.
2. **Spatial subgoals:** choose a target tile/area or building/network anchor; lower level decides edits toward it. Flexible targets but location alone may not communicate whether to expand,repair or remove.
3. **Learned latent modes:** upper level selects a learned option/embedding; lower level interprets it. Fewer prescribed strategy labels, but harder for the user to inspect and not guaranteed to produce meaningful or distinct modes.

A goal-conditioned construction policy and periodic/event-triggered manager is an initial candidate framework, not an approved architecture. Need goal completion/abandonment/interruption and update frequency, what happens during wait/weekly choices, observation/goal encoding, manager versus builder training credit, and how score-only reward is propagated across variable-length goals. Do not automatically add intrinsic goal bonuses or a route generator. Primitive neighboring construction and physical legality rules remain fixed by earlier user decisions. Hierarchy need not mean two separate neural networks.

The top-level choice should cover more than connecting house/destination pairs: modifying shared infrastructure,demolition,resource decisions and traffic management can matter. A house-destination-only vocabulary may limit freedom; proposals must disclose these restrictions rather than calling them physical illegality. Higher-level instructions select objectives,never engineer construction paths.

Primary source inspected: [Bacon/Harb/Precup, Option-Critic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05140), supports joint learned intra-option policies and termination with an option-selection policy; does not show what hierarchy is best for this simulator or establish a drop-in hierarchical MaskablePPO implementation. Sutton/Precup/Singh publisher link returned403 during this turn, so no new claim relies on unread content there. Literature is conceptual evidence, not a game-specific success guarantee.

## GAE lambda .95 challenged during user review

User asks to justify the agent-selected.95. Honest basis: documented SB3 PPO reference default and a conventional initial bias/variance compromise,not project-specific evidence or optimality proof. Original GAE paper supports the tradeoff,not that our task needs.95. With gamma1,each additional decision weights the later TD residual bylambda; .95^20~.3585,.95^100~.00592. Higherlambda such as.99 would give.99^100~.366,but includes more noisy sampled future;lambda1removes lambda decay within available traces but does not solve finite rollout boundaries,poor exploration or missing reward. Critic estimates can propagate value over learning,so do not equate small direct residual weight with permanently forgetting all distant reward.

Paused multi-token construction and required yet undecided hierarchy change the relevant decision horizon. Therefore .95is defensible as a comparison baseline only,not a settled best final value. Reopen D28numeric credit settings pending hierarchy/decision-unit definition and controlled comparison of .95/.99/1 with fixed score objective gamma1,matched scenario/seed budgets and uncertainty. Candidate comparison is discussion,not a run or adopted tuning grid. Source URLs: https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/ppo.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438 . No user approval inferred from challenge; no training or implementation.

## Late complex outcomes and action credit

User asks if the model can distinguish good/bad past choices in late complex situations. Explain statistical credit assignment,not guaranteed causal attribution. PPO/GAE can increase actions associated with outcomes better than critic expectations and decrease those associated with worse outcomes,using later rewards and bootstrapped values. A learned critic can signal expected improvement before trips arrive,but cannot reliably infer never-experienced success from no evidence. An unnecessary road built alongside a useful bridge can receive misleading reinforcement;multiple actions,traffic randomness and later repairs confound blame. Actions are contextual and interactions matter,not universally good/bad labels.

Gamma1does not solve attribution. Lambda,decision unit,rollout boundaries,critic accuracy,exploration and repeated varied experience affect learning;hierarchy may provide useful temporal structure but adds unresolved goal/termination/credit choices and no automatic causal solution. Proposed later diagnostic:save a common simulator state,replay fixed remainder with/without a particular edit where legal,and compare score trajectories across paired seeds;this tests that edit under fixed future behavior,not its universal importance or optimal adaptation. No ablation execution,shaping,curriculum or architecture adopted. Primary conceptual source remains GAEpaper https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438 . No training/new measurement.

## Overtraining concern and refusal to wait

User asks whether excessive training could teach always-act/never-wait behavior. Distinguish overfitting to training seeds,policy/critic instability and action/time/interface degeneration. Never-wait is a possible failure at any training stage,not an inevitable consequence of more steps. With paused edits and trip-only reward,future trips ordinarily require positive game-time advance;correct objective does not award an action-count bonus. No claim this alone guarantees learning to wait. Imperfect critic,exploration collapse,long temporal credit,unbalanced token choices,or faulty handling of external stall truncation can encourage cycling despite absent actual trips. Guard-truncated live-state value bootstrapping is legitimate external-timeout semantics but can expose optimistic value loops;log and diagnose rather than assume stall guard solves learning.

Monitor root-context wait probability,actual positive time advances/complete edit ratio,zero-time token streaks,cancels/noops,stall interruption rate,critic calibration and held-out score/completion alongside training curves. Wait probability must be measured conditional on ROOT and relevant state/offer availability,not all operand stages. There is no universal required waiting percentage;pure waiting can also perform poorly. Fixed validation episodes,replay review and retaining best validation checkpoint detect regression;do not automatically deliver final checkpoint or choose with final heldoutcity. Flat scores require investigation,not automatic proof of overtraining. No forced waits,wait bonuses,edit penalties or strategic masks adopted;preserve user rules. Primary sources revisited:SB3RLtips for overfitting/evaluation/run variability/instability;SB3PPO for entropy and KL/clip controls. Never-wait mechanisms here are engineering hypotheses,not observed model results. No new stopping threshold,userapproval,training or experiment.
