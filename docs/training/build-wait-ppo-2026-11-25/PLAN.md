# Build-or-Wait PPO repair — prepared, not launched

25 November 2026. Approved mini-task repair uncovered a distinct gap: both original and focused-route final models spent all stock in48/48isolated incompatible-pair scenes, despite current inputs containing colors/types. Matching controls16/16. The custom distribution already factors Wait versus conditional road choice. Focused road-only training therefore cannot alter shared-state Wait probability (negative-scene Wait probability≈5%). See ../city-negative-pair-audit-2026-11-25/README.md. This supersedes earlier ordinary-softmax speculation.

Prepare a separate bounded PPO lesson for the decision to act. Keep every conditional-road weight frozen and retain its Adam state; train only the existing global/visual Wait corrections. No new route planner, demonstration actions or supervised behavior-cloning loss. Every action remains Wait or one native legal connection.

Use native initial-state fixtures: matching home/destination, incompatible colors, two homes, two destinations; vary colors, placement and orientation so color itself cannot become a shortcut. Include existing working-network quiet states as Wait cases, and existing partial-network join/route states as build cases, to avoid teaching "any existing road means Wait." Balanced16worker schedule:4matching,2join,2route,2mismatch,1homes,1destinations,4quiet. Native waiting prefixes vary initial time/demand, excluded from learning/reward; no road actions are supplied. These finite teaching tasks do not assert that shared roads are prohibited in live cities.

One decision per episode. Explicit practice reward1 for the appropriate action group (build when this fixture has a connection task; Wait in a negative/quiet fixture), otherwise0. Conditional road choice is frozen: this objective is deliberately about acting versus waiting, not about rewarding a particular route. Native trips and practice reward are recorded separately. Full-city evaluation remains native-score-only. This is a change of mini-task objective, not a change to city reward.

Use Maskable-PPO's clipped on-policy updates. Since each episode is a contextual one-step decision, use zero value predictions during this lesson, gamma0 and value-loss coefficient0; preserve the original city critic weights/Adam unchanged for later export. No city-scale stale value estimates in this binary task. Baseline zero is a valid policy-gradient baseline; this does not remove clipping/minibatch approximation or guarantee transfer. [Policy-gradient baselines, OpenAI Spinning Up](https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/spinningup/rl_intro3.html); [PPO, Schulman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347). These sources support the estimator/objective framework, not our specific lesson or a score guarantee.

Before production: separate native build and ordinary fixture parity; native reset/action and binary-reward contracts; parameter/Adam migration and exact initial actor outputs; one optimizer step changes Wait but preserves conditional road distributions/road weights/old Adam; actual PPO smoke and reload. Use a disposable parent from focusedrun01 for preflight; production parent will be selected after focusedrun02's declared budget and reviews. Preserve all parents/checkpoints. No third production learner while current two studies run.

Initial proposed budget32,768decisions with8k/16k/32kreviews, subject to preflight throughput. Evaluate one-decision group accuracy, repeated60-second native behavior (especially no-build negatives), broader mini tasks and city retention. New training seeds245M, development246M, reserve247M for this lesson's confirmation; original city/mini confirmation236M/237M stays unused. Freeze budget/parent/seed plan before launch.

Risks: one-step classification may not transfer to repeated waiting, congestion or future arrivals. Positive builds are group decisions with frozen placement; they do not establish useful route choice. Already-connected quiet states are not a blanket rule against congestion improvements. Keep conditional roads unchanged, test actual native play and reject regressions. No automatic promotion or overnight launch.
