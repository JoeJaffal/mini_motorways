## 0. Where gp-7 left us

- **New baseline (B7): gp-7 final** (`e8b8b4cad3d5b71c`), test mean 1243.2 on 6100-6999, +256.5 over cma-1.
  This is cma-7's gen-53 mean policy, a CMA-tuned linear rule set.
- **Dynamic GP added nothing.** 7 epochs, 420 proposals, 14 trees raced, 0 inserted. The races were
  underpowered, and 2 of the last 4 raced trees were no-ops.
- **Training kept improving slowly but noisily.** cma-7's gen 73 was no better than its gen 53 on validation
  (+23.7 for gen 53, p 0.46). Both runs ended `budget`.
- **gp-7 final plays "more road".** It builds 258 tiles per game against cma-1's 169, at 4.8 vs 5.8 trips per tile.

## 1. The key fact: game outcomes are mostly chaotic

On the 900 test maps, the correlation between two policies' scores on the same map is about 0.06 (gp-7 vs
cma-1) and 0.08 (gp-7 vs hand). The map seed fixes the start, but small decision differences snowball (house
spawns, overflows), so pairing by map removes almost no variance.

Consequences:
- Every comparison (selection in CMA, GP races, validation, test) costs roughly what an *unpaired* one would.
  Paired SD is about 440 per game.
- Selection on 12 maps per generation is mostly luck (reliability about 0.3). Detecting +30 needs about 350 maps.
- Apparent per-map patterns (for example "gains most where cma-1 did badly") are mostly regression to the mean.

**This is the first thing to attack, because it limits everything else.**

## 2. Work streams (draft)

### A. Make evaluation cheaper per unit of information
- **Why it happens (code, f34):** the simulator has one random stream (`TrafficWorld.random`) shared by house/shop
  placement, demand, upgrade offers and every car's slot choice. Car choices depend on the roads, so one different
  road shifts the stream and every later spawn changes.
- **A1. Keyed random draws in the simulator (decided 2026-11-20: build and measure).**
  - **f34 baseline (committed data, no new games):**
    - same-seed correlation of two policies' trips: gp-7 final vs L 0.06 on test, 0.12 on validation;
      cma-7 gen 53 vs gen 73 (a near pair) 0.13; x0\* vs cma-7 final 0.02;
    - among the 24 candidates of one generation, the seed explains a median 11% of variance (74 generations).
  - **Design.** A stream per event type is not enough: house and shop placement draws once per *valid* candidate
    cell (`OriginalPlacement.GeneratePlacements`), and validity depends on the roads. So every draw is keyed by
    what it is for: hash(map seed, event type, event number, cell or slot) with a counter-based generator such as
    SplitMix64. The same spot on the same map gets the same number whatever the roads are.
    - Keyed: house/shop placement weights, suburb (grouping) and colour draws, shop schedule jitter, demand phases,
      weekly upgrade offers (draw = hash(seed, "offer", week, pick)), and car slot choices (hash of seed, car,
      trip number).
    - It is a **new simulator version** (working name f35-keyed), selected by a flag; f34 stays the default and
      byte-identical. Built in a new worktree; `mm-gp6-final` and the main checkout's Release DLL are never rebuilt.
  - **Gates before any measurement:**
    - f34 mode unchanged: 10 committed LA test seeds (6100-6109) replayed by the new build in f34 mode match
      `final/gp7/games/test_6100-6999.csv` exactly for gp-7 final, L and hand.
    - keyed mode deterministic: 5 seeds repeated are identical.
    - The laptop Unity replay build needs the same change before keyed games can be watched.
  - **Measurement (pre-set before any game):** on 100 fresh LA seeds, play a near pair (cma-7 gen 53 vs gen 73)
    and a far pair (gp-7 final vs L) on f34 and on keyed.
    **Pass = the near pair's paired SD on keyed ≤ 0.7 × its f34 paired SD** (that is, at least 2× fewer seeds for
    the same precision). The same-seed correlation is reported with it. If it fails, gp-8 stays on f34.
  - StrokeQ notified 2026-11-20 (new version alongside f34; f34 and mm-gp6-final untouched).
- **A2. Variance-aware evaluation.** For example, shorter games (cap below 3600 s) as a cheap proxy, if the
  correlation with full games is high; or adaptive racing (add maps only to close candidates).
- **A3. Re-measure the noise** (same-map correlation, paired SD, selection reliability) after A1/A2, before
  choosing λ, maps per generation and race sizes.

### B. Study the stats (from existing data first, no new games)
- What ends games: which shop overflows, when, and what the policy was doing just before (decision logs, replays).
- Where tiles, upgrades and waiting go; why gp-7's trips per tile are lower.
- What separates high and low games *of the same policy* (since map difficulty explains little).
- Output: 3-5 concrete weaknesses, each turned into a feature, a planner change or a GP target.

### C. Rebuild dynamic GP
- Screen: only keep trees whose w0 survives text rounding; skip no-op arms; remove the slot-3 bias.
- Race: size it from the A3 noise numbers (or use CRN); consider sequential testing.
- Proposals: seed them from the stream-B weaknesses instead of random trees.

### D. Lights, roundabouts, motorways
- What each adds, what the simulator and planner need, and how to test each fairly against B7.
- Last in order (decided 2026-11-20). With a 1-week budget, probably design only in gp-8.

### E. Other cities (decided 2026-11-20)
- Until now every game has been Los Angeles (all training, validation and test). The simulator also has Beijing,
  Tokyo, Dar es Salaam and London (`CityCatalog.cs`), never used or checked by us.
- **Step 1, smoke:** 1-2 verified games per city with gp-7 final. Which cities run without errors? LA's profile has
  no stations or rails, the others may. The planner's 50×30 grid must fit each map.
- **Step 2, descriptive check (f34, not a decision):** gp-7 final, L (cma-1) and hand on 100 seeds per working city,
  seeds 8000-8099 Beijing, 8100-8199 Tokyo, 8200-8299 Dar es Salaam, 8300-8399 London (unplayed; StrokeQ told).
  London is excluded for now: the smoke game failed (see the log).
  Report mean, median and best per city, and the paired gaps. ±90 trips resolution per city; it answers "does the
  ranking hold off LA?", not "by how much".
- Needs `--city` in evaluate/battery (CODE_VERSION bump), carried into cache keys and run names. Gate: a few LA
  seeds replayed under the new code match the committed CSV for all three policies.
- About 1,200 games ≈ 4-5 hours at 10 workers.
