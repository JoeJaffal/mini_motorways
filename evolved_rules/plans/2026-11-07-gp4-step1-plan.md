# gp-4 step 1: lights and roundabouts as planner moves, and the hand tests (implementation plan)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the planner can place traffic lights and roundabouts, decided by a `place` score on the policy. Hand rules that use these moves are measured on LA 3100–3199 and reported to the user before any evolution runs.

**Architecture:**
- A new module `placement.py` lists placement spots (lights on junctions, roundabout footprints) and their side effects.
- `features.py` describes each spot. `agent.decide` gains a placement step after routing, gated by the policy's `places_lights` / `places_roundabouts` flags.
- Routing learns roundabouts: the centre is blocked and the ring is one-way.
- Old policies never reach any new code path, so they play exactly the same games.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12 (`.venv`), numpy, pytest, and the headless C# host (Release `TrafficHost.dll`, already built; do not rebuild).

**Spec:** `evolved_rules/plans/2026-11-07-gp4-lights-roundabouts.md` (approved 2026-11-07). Read it first.

**Scope:** design step 1 only. The 5-tree GP text, the evolution run, CMA-ES and the final test (steps 2–4) get their own plan after the user sees step 1's results.
- **No `CODE_VERSION` bump here.** Tree texts play exactly as before in step 1 (Task 5 pins this), so the engine cache stays valid. Step 2 bumps it when 5-tree texts arrive.
- The `place` decision is a Python method in step 1 (`hand_place`). The GP `place` tree and its scaled terminals come in step 2, reading the `pl_*` features from Task 4.

### Task 3: Placement candidates

**Files:**
- Create: `evolved_rules/placement.py`
- Test: `evolved_rules/tests/test_gp4.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `candidates.link_degree`, `candidates.doorsteps`, `candidates.near_cells`, `candidates._served_colours`, `candidates.DOORSTEP_RADIUS`, `CityState.component`, `CityState.centres()`, `features.STUCK_SPEED`.
- Produces:
  - `LIGHT = "TrafficLight"`, `ROUNDABOUT = "Roundabout"`, `PLACE_CAP = 32`, `PLACE_RADIUS = 2`.
  - `@dataclass(frozen=True) PlacementCandidate`: `tool: str`, `cell: Cell` (the light cell or the roundabout centre), `footprint: tuple[Cell, ...]` (the cell, or centre + ring in N, W, S, E order), `degree: int` (highest `link_degree` over the footprint), `new_links: int`, `joins_separated: bool`, `tiles_lost: int`.
  - `ring_of(centre: Cell) -> tuple[Cell, Cell, Cell, Cell]`.
  - `ring_new_links(state, centre) -> list[tuple[Cell, Cell]]`.
  - `placement_candidates(state: CityState, lights: bool, roundabouts: bool) -> list[PlacementCandidate]`.

**Rules (from the spec, mirroring the host):**
- **Light spot:** infrastructure 1, not pending removal, playable, `link_degree >= 3`.
- **Roundabout spot:** centre and ring all in the grid, playable, terrain land (not 1 or 2), building kind 0, not infrastructure 3 or 4, not pending removal. Mirrors `AddRoundabout`, TrafficWorld.cs:185–188.
- **Excluded roundabout spots:** any footprint cell equal to a building's outside cell (`Building.outside` of every house and shop).
- **Useful roundabout spots only:** some footprint cell has `link_degree >= 3`, or lies within `DOORSTEP_RADIUS` of a doorstep.
- `ring_new_links`: for each ring cell and each of the 8 directions, a neighbour that is in the grid, not in the footprint, has infrastructure ≠ 0, is not infrastructure 3, and is not already linked to the ring cell. Mirrors TrafficWorld.cs:198–203. `new_links = len(...)`.
- `joins_separated`: collect the networks (`state.component`) of every road cell in the footprint and every new-link neighbour. It is true when there are 2 or more distinct networks and the union of colours they serve (`_served_colours`) has 2 or more colours.
- `tiles_lost`: footprint cells with infrastructure 1 or 2.
- **Order:** by (−stopped cars within `PLACE_RADIUS` of any footprint cell, −cars within it, tool (light before roundabout), y, x). Then cut to `PLACE_CAP`.
  - A car is "stopped" when it is driving (stage not Home or Parking) with `speed < STUCK_SPEED`.
  - A car belongs to the cell it is in (floor of x, y).

  - `pl_new_links`, `pl_joins_separated`, `pl_tiles_lost`: from the candidate.
  - `pl_light_stock`, `pl_round_stock`: inventory indices 3 and 4.
