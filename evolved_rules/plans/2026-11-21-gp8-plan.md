## 1. What gp-8 is

cma-7's method (sep-CMA-ES over the linear rule weights and the planner), changed in three ways:
1. **Simulator f35-keyed** (`2026-11-21.f35-keyed`): A1 passed its pre-set rule (near-pair paired SD × 0.62).
2. **Four training cities** (user, 2026-11-21: all four, none held out): Los Angeles, Beijing, Tokyo, Dar es
   Salaam. London is out (its smoke game fails); once fixed it is the unseen-city check.
3. **Traffic lights and roundabouts**, each with its own gated head that starts nearly shut (section 3).

Dynamic GP is not in gp-8 (moved to gp-9). B7 = gp-7 final (`e8b8b4cad3d5b71c`), the baseline. L = cma-1 3-tree.

## 2. Code and gates

Code `gp8-v1` (commit fa8cd51 and later, frozen and tagged `cma8-launch` before launch):
- engine and evolve7 carry the city (map id = city index × 1,000,000 + seed; LA ids are plain seeds, so old keys
  are unchanged) and the randomness (part of the cache key; a game on the wrong simulator, city or seed aborts).
- A placing policy's placement checks are placement-only steps: no offers or routes, and the decision clock does
  not move. So a policy whose heads never fire plays exactly the games of the same policy without tools.
- New placement input `pl_placed` (this kind's placements so far); `time` joins the head terms; a roundabout head.

## 3. Lights and roundabouts heads

place = if_gt(pl_is_light, .5, light head, if_gt(pl_is_roundabout, .5, round head, −1)); a tool is placed when
its head's best spot scores above 0, at most one per check, checks at most every 5 s while a driving car has
waited 3 s and the tool is in stock.
- Each head: bias + 10 terms (degree, doorstep, cars near, stopped near, max and mean wait near, distance to the
  worst shop, worst-colour cars stuck, placed so far, time) + the light template. All are CMA coordinates.
- Start: bias B, stopped near +1, max wait near +1, placed −1 (x units; scales in `ops/x0_gp8.py`).
  **B = −4 for both heads.** Probe (seeds 9002-9004, 3 per city, keyed): at B = −3.5 and −4 the roundabout head
  placed in 7 of 12 games (more than the 10-30% aimed at), with mean trips +8.0 and −5.8 vs B7 (noise level);
  several placements came late in failing games. B = −3 placed in 4 of 8 games (9000-9001).
- **B7 never takes a light offer** (41 offered in 12 games, 0 taken; roundabouts taken in most games), so the light
  head starts without stock. The upgrade weights for light and roundabout offers (type_3, type_4) stay rule
  coordinates starting at B7's values, but start at the full std (`--tool-offer-std`), not 0.25×, so the search
  can learn to stock lights.

## 5. Evaluation (fixed now, before any training game)

- Seed blocks, per city (all four cities): validation 9500-9549 (200 maps), test 9600-9799 (800 maps).
  Smoke and calibration use 9000-9019 and 9900-9999, never validation or test.
- Pre-played on f35-keyed before freeze: B7 and L on validation and test (2,000 games), split across machines.
- Freeze at stop: C\* = cma-8's final in-bounds mean. There is one challenger, so validation does not pick.
- **Headline:** C\* − B7 on the 800 test maps pooled, one-sided paired t, α 0.05, looked at once.
- **Secondaries** (Holm): C\* − B7 per city on test (4, two-sided); tools ablation, C\* − (C\* with lights and
  roundabouts off) on validation (two-sided). One training arm (user, 2026-11-21): the ablation answers "do the
  tools help this policy", not "did training with tools help".
- Reported with each: mean, median, best, worst; lights and roundabouts per game; mean car wait; rejected actions.
