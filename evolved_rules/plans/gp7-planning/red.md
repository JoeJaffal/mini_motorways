[major] (§3.3 outer loop: slot freeing and operator credit) The slot 'signal ratio' I_k = |m_k|/(σ√C_kk) is not a signal statistic under noise. A coordinate with zero true effect random-walks, and I_k grows with the slot's age. Freeing and credit therefore track age and drift, not value.
  evidence: Under pure noise, m_k ~ N(0, σ²C_kk·g/μ_eff), so I_k ~ |N(0, g/μ_eff)|. With μ_eff ≈ 3.7: P(I_k < 1 | zero signal) is 0.46 at 10 generations, 0.38 at 15 and 0.33 at 20 (rt_stats/calc.py). Older useless slots therefore survive more often. cma-6 showed this kind of drift: 6.44 against 5.82 expected from a pure random walk, and leave-one-generation-out ridge R² < 0. Operator credit ('survives 2 epochs with I > 1') inherits the same bias.
  fix: Replace I_k with a test that has a known null. For example, accumulate per generation the rank correlation between candidates' z_k and their fitness ranks, and compare the sum against a permutation null; or regress centred candidate scores on z_k over the epoch. Free a slot only when its effect e_k is small and its accumulated z-score has |z| < 1. Base credit on that statistic, not on I_k.
